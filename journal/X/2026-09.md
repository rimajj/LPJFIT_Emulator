## 2026-09-08 — the third pre-registration, and two defects found by gating first

Task was "write the third pre-registration: the held-out forcing leg", with a standing instruction
to gate the build provenance of the low-emissions leg first. Gating first is what made the session:
both real findings came out of the gate, not out of the pre-registration.

**The build gate.** Provenance comes from the model's own log banner, since a restart file carries
no build stamp. Three builds, not two. The Feb-05 → Aug-12 difference is 19 uncommitted files in a
source tree whose newest commit predates both, and reading all of them: every behavioural change is
behind a rung-2 environment variable the job scripts do not set, the two new tree fields are
deliberately not serialised, and `gasdev.c` changed only by hoisting a static, so the random stream
is untouched. Two corroborations from the other end: the reader parses both legs identically, and
the two legs' two-seed spreads agree to within 6 %. Verdict: admissible, byte-equality still
unproven.

**The defect the gate turned up.** The high-emissions leg's two seed tables are byte-identical —
all 22 quantities, all 67,420 cells. The `random_seed2` run starts from historical *seed 1* and
reads its random numbers from there. So that leg's "two-seed mean" is one draw, and a band derived
from it is the bare 10 % floor wearing the costume of `max(10 %, spread)`. The sealed kill test's
verdict is unaffected (−0.727 against a gate of +0.050) and was NOT re-run.

**The pre-registration.** Persistence — hand back the cell's own 1999 forest — is the decisive null
at 0.033749, so the bar is 0.0837. The design contribution is the non-circular band: tolerance from
a different leg than the truth, which drops the single-realisation ceiling from 1.0 to 0.538490.
All five nulls derived before sealing, on SLURM, with a ledger row.

**Three process mistakes, all mine, all instructive.**

1. I sealed before bringing the line up to date with `main`, 29 commits behind. A seal freezes
   *every* claim, including "no wrapper exists for the byte test" — which was false on main. The

   record. Rebasing afterwards then orphaned the seal commit the registry names; fixed by appending

2. I merged `main` into the line before thinking; the commit guard refused it, because a merge
   commit makes every path main touched look like a write by this line. Aborted and rebased instead.
3. **A false green of my own making.** I checked formatting with `grep -c "^Would reformat"`, a
