### Added

- **First whole-globe test of the carbon-matched restart against the right reference: fail, but year 1 is already as good as a real run.** The owner agreed that a restart continued in the real model is compared with a real run of the model averaged over the same number of years (decision record `20260925-INT-a-continuation-is-judged-against-a-real-run-on-the-same-window`). Sealed test `X-20260925-continuation-same-window`, 20,123 held-out cells: over the first 10 years the model started from the new restart lands inside the band in 49.4 % of cells, against 62.2 % for a real run, so it fails the rule (it may trail by at most 2 points). It beats every baseline: continuing the stored 1999 state gets 28.2 %, and the previous emulated restart 27.3 %. In its first year alone it matches a real year. What pulls it down is a die-off in year 5, when global vegetation carbon falls from 678 to 607 PgC. Two fixes tried on a 51-range sample did not help: resetting the trees' inherited stress counters made the die-off worse, and placing fewer, taller trees changed nothing.
