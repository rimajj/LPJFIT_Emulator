### Added

- **What limits the vegetation-carbon map for the stored spin-up: how many distinct places it has learned from, not how densely.** A learning curve on the development cells (36,863 tree-bearing cells, not a sealed test) shows the map lands in the band in 60.5 % of cells trained on everything, 48-52 % trained on half the 15-degree regions, but still 57-59 % trained on half the cells of every region. Error falls roughly as the number of regions covered to the power -0.59, and only as the power -0.14 of the density inside them. To reach a rerun (85 % in the band) each cell's error has to fall to about 0.4 of today's, which on that slope needs roughly five times as many distinct climate situations as the land surface offers. Neighbouring cells with near-identical inputs agree with each other less well than a rerun of one cell does (79 % against 90 %), and the map already predicts their differences as well as a model fitted to the differences themselves. Two new read-only scripts: `diag_spinup_vegc_learning_curve.py` and `diag_spinup_vegc_twins.py`.
