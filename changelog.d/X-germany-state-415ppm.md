### Added

- **The worst Germany climate was traced to two things in the owner's runs, and neither explains why the emulator falls short.** The MPI-ESM1-2-HR ssp245 runs (both seeds) read a different CO₂ file and held 401.63 ppm after 2100 where every other run held 415.78; since the emulator must not respond to CO₂, that run is a different target. Separately, the ssp245 runs' newer model build fixes how new trees inherit wood density and maximum rooting depth; checked on every cell of all twelve year-3070 states, the old build leaves 3-4 % of trees outside their own type's allowed range and the new one almost none, but it moves only the extreme tails, not the 10th-90th percentiles the test scores. Re-running the full-forest test on the five runs at 415.78 ppm (pre-registered and sealed first) still fails, and by more: 43.0 % of cell-climate pairs fully in band against a second run's 49.2 % (the rule needed 47.2 %). Removing the odd run took away the nearest training climate of MPI ssp370, whose score halved (44.8 % to 22.4 %). The limit is how few distinct climates the map is trained on, not the CO₂ slip or the build. New: `scripts/diag_germany_build_fingerprint.py`, and `--exclude` in `scripts/exp_germany_state.py`.
