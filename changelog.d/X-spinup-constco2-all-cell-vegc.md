### Added
- **An all-cell test of the emulator against the stored spin-up's constant-CO2 years, as the owner asked on 2026-09-24.** The stored global spin-up recycles the 1901-1930 climate and holds CO2 at 276.59 ppm until model year 1699; of those years it kept, per cell, only vegetation carbon (both seeds, every year). `scripts/spinup_target.py` turns that into a per-cell equilibrium target (the 1200-1699 mean, its two halves, its trend), the 1901-1930 climate inputs for every cell, the same window statistics for all 12,000 pilot runs from their own output files, and a check that the stored series counts grass as well as trees (trees hold a median 91 % of vegetation carbon in tree-bearing cells, but under 90 % in about half of them). `scripts/exp_spinup_vegc.py` scores a prediction the way the owner's pass rule reads: against each seed, with a band whose width comes from an earlier, separate stretch of the same two runs, and compared with how often a second run of the model lands inside that band (85.9 % of the 56,986 cells). Two pre-registrations use it: one for the emulator trained only on the pilot, one for a map trained on the stored spin-up's own cells.
