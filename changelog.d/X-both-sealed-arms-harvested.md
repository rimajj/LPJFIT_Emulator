### Added

- **The first positive result in this project: the emulator CAN predict how a forest changes when the climate warms — where that question is identifiable.** `X-20260909-pilot-warming-response` **passes**, scoring **0.545304** against a best information-free competitor of 0.145690 and a pre-registered bar of 0.225690, a margin of **+0.399614**. It beats the best competitor at **all 29 of 29 perturbation levels** (margins +0.114915 to +0.609290), so the non-monotonicity clause written into the sealed estimand is *satisfied* rather than waived, and at 5° blocking instead of 15° it scores 0.552327, so the conclusion does not turn on the blocking radius. All seven competitors reproduced their sealed values exactly, so the apparatus that produced the number is the apparatus that was sealed on 2026-09-09. ⚠ **Quote it as 63 % of attainable, never as a fraction of 1.0** — the ceiling is a measured third model seed at 0.869730, itself a lower bound because a cell's two arms share a random seed.

- **A composition kill test is sealed** as `X-20260914-pilot-composition-response`: can the emulator predict how a cell's *mix of tree types* shifts? Seven competitors, bar **0.337858** against an attainable **0.863852**. The apparatus was derived by line T and held unrun pending the seal.

### Fixed

- **The pre-registered competitor set for the response test was complete and still missed a whole class, and the verdict now says so.** A model **blinded** to which of the 29 perturbations it is asked about scores **0.349462 — above the pass bar of 0.225690.** Every competitor that was pre-registered is information-free *by construction*, so none of them was a *learned-but-treatment-blind* rival, and without that arm beside it the 0.545304 reads as far more response skill than was demonstrated. **The pass survives the stricter test**: scored against the blind arm as though it had been the best competitor the margin is **+0.195842**, still clearing the pre-registered 0.080, and scrambling the forcing *independently per cell* scores 0.308049 — *below* blind — so removing the forcing hurts less than lying about it, i.e. the model genuinely reads the climate. These arms are diagnostics, recorded outside the decision rule. ⚠ **The scramble arm was broken on its first build and the broken version looked like a near-miss** (0.4950 against 0.5453): the 29 design points are identical at every cell, so one shared permutation is a pure relabelling the model relearns under new names. **Draw a scramble arm per unit.**

- **A pre-registered threshold was about to be inherited that the best competitor satisfies by itself.** The composition test was proposed with the same +0.080 margin the response test uses. Under the `model_minus_best_null` comparator the no-power rule scores each competitor against the best of the *others*, so what constrains the threshold is the gap between the best competitor and the **runner-up** — here **0.143526** at 15° and **0.148198** at 5°. At +0.080 the best competitor clears the bar unaided, at both radii, and the experiment would have been `invalid` by construction on the day it was sealed. The sealed threshold is **+0.160**, which no competitor satisfies at either radius. ⚠ **A large separation is the bar, not free headroom** — it was offered as evidence the test was well-powered, and it is, but it raises the threshold by exactly the same amount.

- **The seal-provenance check failed on a seal that was never touched, because rebasing orphans the commit it cites.** `experiments/registry.jsonl` records `seal_commit` as a bare git hash, but the runbook mandates a rebase onto main before merging, which rewrites a line's commits — so the recorded hash points at an orphan and the "the seal existed before the run" check reports a violation on provenance that is intact. Verified intact here three ways: the pre-registration's content hash still matches the file, the file is byte-identical to its version in the seal commit as it now exists, and that commit *is* an ancestor of the result it governs. Corrected as the ledger's own error text prescribes, by **appending a superseding row rather than editing one**, since the registry is append-only and its reader already takes the last row per experiment. Two such rows now exist for the same cause; flagged for the integrator to resolve the seal by content or by a rebase-surviving ref.

### Changed

- **The held-out forcing leg fails, and the failure is the informative kind.** `X-20260908-heldout-forcing-leg` returns **0.005443** where simply handing back a cell's own present-day forest returns **0.033749** — beaten by persistence six-fold, a margin of −0.028306 against a required +0.050. This is pre-named outcome **(c)**, "actively harmed by a climate it has not seen": at 0.005443 the model sits *between* `geographic_address` (0.005426) and `nearest_analogue` (0.004688), so statistically **it is the copy-a-neighbour competitor**. The sealed document pre-committed that only outcome (a) would license continuing to look for a warming response in that corpus — so this directs the search to the designed perturbation ensemble, which is exactly where it was found. ⚠ **This does not license "the emulator cannot learn a warming response."** The two results are consistent and the reconciliation is identification, not model quality: the ensemble spins one cell up under 30 climates so the response is separated by construction, while in the scenario legs every cell holds exactly one climate, so climate and geography are collinear and the response is not identified at all. **Read 0.005443 against 0.538490** — one draw of the real model — so the model reaches 1.0 % of attainable and persistence 6.3 %. Apparatus validation, worth stating: the same run scored the map on the leg it was *fitted* on and returned 0.036067, reproducing the 0.0361 already recorded for that experiment.

- **One earlier sealed result has its basis corrected, and it is the only one the byte-clone reaches.** `X-20260908-warming-response` declares its truth as "the mean of that leg's two seeds"; the configured second seed was a bit-identical copy of the first, so it was a single realisation duplicated. All arms share that truth and every competitor returned its pre-registered value, and −0.727 against a competitor pinned analytically at 0.000 is not a margin a noise-level change could close, so **the failure stands**. ⚠ **But the reason that leg was chosen does not survive the fix**: the basis names build-matching as why the high-emissions leg is primary, which held only because the second seed was a copy — the genuine one comes from a later binary build, so a re-run straddles a build boundary and loses the property that motivated the choice. Not a drop-in repoint. The other sealed results are untouched: the held-out leg declares that leg unused in any arm, and the ensemble uses its own runs and no such band.
